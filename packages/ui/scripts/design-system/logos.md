# Logos

The Shifa logo, "Jasmine Cross", copied from the repository (`docs/brand/logo/`). All text is outlined. Colour versions: petals leaf green `#1f6f5c` and night green `#134a3d`, dot door gold `#c8a24a`.

- `*-color`: on cream or white (`shifa-color-bg-app`, `shifa-color-bg-surface`).
- `*-reverse`: on night green or the dark theme.
- `*-white`: white on leaf green or photos.
- `*-mono`: one ink (night green) for thermal printers, stamps and fax.
- `shifa-app-icon`, `favicon`: launcher and browser tab.

Clear space: twice the gold dot's diameter on every side. Never stretch, recolour, rotate or add effects.
