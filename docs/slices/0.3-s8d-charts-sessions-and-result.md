# 0.3-S8d Charts spike: doctor sessions, revision round, report

Status: Planned (owner approved the plan, 2026-11-17) · Tier: T1 (confirms or changes clinical chart rules in the dental and orthopedics domain docs; real doctors take part, identified only by a code) · Branch: `chore/0.3-s8d-charts-sessions-and-result`
Spec: [0.3-S8 Charts](../specs/spikes/0.3-s8-charts.md) criteria 10–13 · Requirements: DEN-03, DEN-04, DEN-05, DEN-09, DEN-15, DEN-19, ORT-01, ORT-02, ORT-03, ORT-05 (confirmed or changed) · Decisions: ADR-0011 (unchanged), ADR-0023, ADR-0026, OD-17, OD-19 and OD-20 (input only, stay open), OD-47
After [0.3-S8c](0.3-s8c-charts-perio-skeleton-and-session-kit.md). The session starts when the owner has the exported logs and moderator notes of the five sessions, so no branch waits for doctors' calendars. Built in three checkpoints (A–C); B exists only if a chart misses the pass mark.

## Goal
The owner reads one report that says, with numbers recomputed from the committed logs, which chart rules five local doctors confirmed, which must change and what Phase B and C must build, and whether Claude Design should also replace Figma for the design system.

## Choices made by this plan
- **Before the session (owner):** five in-person sessions from `docs/spikes/s8-moderator-script.md` on the build merged by 0.3-S8c: 3 dentists (about 45 minutes), 2 orthopedic surgeons (about 25 minutes); one exported file per device and the filled notes template per participant.
- **What enters the repository:** `docs/spikes/s8-logs/<code>.json` (the per-device exports merged by the script) and `docs/spikes/s8-logs/<code>-notes.md` (comments rewritten by the owner without names, clinics, places or anything that identifies a doctor; ADR-0026). A test refuses a log that fails the schema or a notes file with a phone-like digit run.
- **Numbers come from a script, never by hand:** `pnpm --filter @shifa-spike/s8-rules report docs/spikes/s8-logs` prints the per-task and per-chart tables (attempts, unassisted successes, assisted, not attempted, median and range of duration, undo, wrong-tooth and wrong-side counts, keyboard vs keypad for perio) with the pass-mark function of 0.3-S8a; the report embeds its output between markers and a test fails when they differ.
- **Revision round (criterion 12) stays in this slice:** a chart that misses gets one design revision (in the Claude Design project first, then in `spikes/s8-charts/web`), pushed on this branch; the owner retests its failed tasks with at least two participants (new ones where possible; repeats named) on that commit, and the retest logs are committed as `<code>-r2.json`. A second miss is reported as a failure and carried to Phase B or C in the roadmap. If the revision would exceed about 800 lines, it becomes its own slice and the owner is told.
- **Five participants are a small sample:** the report gives counts, not percentages alone, and says so wherever a conclusion rests on one or two people.

## Plan
1. Contracts, database, API, web, Flutter: none unless checkpoint B runs.
2. **A. Logs and numbers (criteria 9–11):** merge and validate the exports, commit logs and notes, report script and its tests, first tables; decide per chart: pass, or revision.
3. **B. Revision and retest (criterion 12, conditional):** revision of the failed chart, its Playwright suites kept green, owner retest, retest logs.
4. **C. Report and docs (criterion 13):** `docs/spikes/s8-charts.md` with one section per spec question (tooth-first, two palettes and Missing/Extracted against the 43% baseline; the bridge wizard; perio time by keyboard and keypad and the expected path; wrong side or level and the side chip; touch on the tablet; Arabic terms doctors use for surfaces, statuses, symptom types and sides; what doctors said about digits, as input to OD-20), the build commit(s), limits of the sample, and the advice on Claude Design vs Figma for the design system (a change needs a new ADR superseding the Figma line of ADR-0011 and closes OD-19; this slice writes no such ADR). `docs/domain/dental.md` and `docs/domain/orthopedics.md` get the confirmed and changed rules and their verification notes; `docs/open-decisions.md` gets the evidence under OD-19 and OD-20; roadmap and the four slice files updated.

## Tests written first
- `rules/test/report.test.ts` (10–12): fixture logs with known outcomes give the expected tables; assisted attempts and early stops are counted as specified; a retest file replaces only the retested tasks and names repeat participants; merging two device files keeps every attempt once.
- `rules/test/committed-logs.test.ts` (9, 13): every file in `docs/spikes/s8-logs/` validates against the schema; participant codes are within `D1`–`D3`, `O1`–`O2` (plus retest codes); notes files hold no digit run of 8 or more; the tables embedded in the report equal the script's output. Runs in the `rules` job of the `s8-charts` workflow.

Estimated hand-written lines: ~1,200 without a revision (budget ~4,000). Report script ~250; tests and fixtures ~250; report ~400; domain docs, open decisions, roadmap and slice files ~300. A revision adds up to ~800.
