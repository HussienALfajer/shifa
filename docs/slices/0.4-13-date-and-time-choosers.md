Status: Built (PR open) · Tier: T1 · Branch: `feat/0.4-13-date-and-time-choosers`
Spec: [0.4 follow-up before Phase A](../specs/design-system/0.4-followup-before-phase-a.md) criteria 14–19 · Requirements: none · Decisions: OD-20, OD-61, OD-62, OD-68 (B), OD-69 (A), OD-70 (B), ADR-0039, ADR-0040

Follows [0.4-12](0.4-12-narrow-width-and-small-items.md) (merged, #122). One slice by the owner's choice (2026-11-23), ~4,500 hand-written lines, above the ADR-0032 budget (CI warns, never fails); five checkpoints, each committed green.

Builds from the approved sheet "Shifa Components (proposals)", page "Before Phase A · 0.4-12 and 0.4-13" (approved 2026-11-23): the Date Picker month and year panel, the typed date with its error texts, and the Time Input slot grid. Read those boards before checkpoint 2 and take sizes, spacing and the slot-group boundaries from them. No sheet checkpoint is needed; the slice ends with the design-system sync (ADR-0040).

## Goal
A receptionist reaches a birth date decades back in two presses (heading → year → month), types a date segment by segment with Arabic error texts that never guess a two-digit year, and picks an appointment time from the clinic's 15-minute slots with one press; typing stays the main way in both fields.

## Plan
No contracts, database, API, Flutter or end-to-end changes (the Flutter choosers are out of scope). Everything is in `packages/ui`: `src/date-picker/` and `src/time-input/`. Files stay under 400 lines: new parts go in their own files.

### Checkpoint 1 — UI SDK `DateInput` RTL gate (criterion 18)
- `src/date-picker/date-input.gate.test.tsx`, in both directions, on UI SDK's `DateInput` with explicit `DateInputSegment` children (the order our wrapper will force, not the locale's): segments render day / month / year left to right in RTL and LTR (`dir="ltr"` on the group, as Time Input does); Left and Right move to the segment on that side; Up/Down step and wrap; a digit that cannot start a two-digit value moves on («4» → 04); `/`, `-`, `.` and space move on; Backspace in an empty segment goes back; pasting `14/03/1987`, `14-3-1987`, `14.3.1987` and `1987-03-14` into any segment fills all three; Arabic-Indic digits (through `useArabicIndicKeys`, as Time Input); the value is ISO and `null` until complete; the partial state (which segments are empty) can be read from `state.segments` for criterion 16's «السنة ناقصة»; a two-digit year is not expanded to 19xx/20xx.
- Check first whether `DateInput` lets us force the day / month / year order whatever the locale (a locale such as `en-GB` already orders it so; `ar-SY-u-nu-latn` must be checked), and whether its segments take `aria-label` (Arabic names «اليوم»، «الشهر»، «السنة») and `placeholder` («يوم»، «شهر»، «سنة»).
- Outcome, recorded in this file under "Gate result": each check passes on UI SDK, passes through a wrapper fix (name the fix, as the Date Picker digit fix in 0.4-04), or fails. If a core behaviour fails (order, paste, partial state, two-digit year guessing), the segments are built on Time Input's parts as the spec says (`TimeInputSegment` and a small date controller of our own); stop and tell the owner the line estimate before going on.

### Checkpoint 2 — segmented entry, errors and sync (criteria 15–17)

Named spinbuttons, placeholders, `autoComplete` from the Field (OD-67, `bday` opt-in). Paste handling if checkpoint 1 needs a wrapper: `src/date-picker/parse-date.ts` (pure: the four formats, Arabic-Indic digits, returns `{day, month, year}` or a reason).
- `src/date-picker/date-errors.ts` (pure): one function from (segments, min, max, dir, `maxMessage`) to an error text or none. Texts: «شباط 1987 فيه 28 يوماً» (29/02 on a non-leap year, 31 in a 30-day month), «أكمل التاريخ: السنة ناقصة» (and الشهر / اليوم), «اختر تاريخاً من 23/11/2026 فصاعداً», «اختر تاريخاً حتى …», `maxMessage` override («تاريخ الميلاد لا يكون بعد اليوم»), «اكتب السنة بأربعة أرقام: 1987»; English texts in LTR. Shown on blur of the group, or at once when a complete date is impossible; the `error` prop from a form wins over it.

- Sync: `DatePicker`'s `month` is controlled by the wrapper: a complete typed date sets the calendar's month; a chosen day fills the segments (one value, owned by `BaseDatePicker`; the segments follow `value`).
- Tests first (browser, both directions): `date-picker.segments.test.tsx` (order left to right, names and placeholders, «14031987» → 14/03/1987, «4» moves on, separators, Backspace back, Up/Down, Arabic-Indic digits, the four paste formats, ISO value, `bday` opt-in); `date-errors.test.ts` (every text in both languages, leap years, 30-day months, min/max, `maxMessage`, two-digit year); `date-picker.sync.test.tsx` (typed date opens the calendar on its month; a chosen day fills the segments; weekday line). ### Checkpoint 3 — month and year panel (criterion 14)
- `src/date-picker/month-year-panel.tsx`: the heading becomes a button («تشرين الثاني 2026» + caret, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`); it swaps the day grid for one panel inside the same popover: a year listbox at the logical start (scrolled to the chosen year or the current one) beside a 3 × 4 grid of Levantine month names (`DATE_LOCALE`, OD-61). Choosing a month sets the calendar's `month` and shows its days.
- Year range (spec edge case): both given → `min`'s year to `max`'s year; only `min` → `min`'s year to today + 10; only `max` → `max` − 120 years to `max`; neither → 1900 to today + 10.
- Keys: in the year list Up/Down one year, Page Up/Down ten, Home/End first and last, typed digits jump to a year (buffered, as typeahead); in the month grid arrows follow the reading direction; Enter chooses; Escape goes back one step (months → years → days) and never closes the popover (`onDismissAttempt` or a capture-phase handler; check `date-picker.d.ts`). Years and months outside `min`/`max` are `aria-disabled`, still read and focusable, as disabled days are.
- Check whether `CalendarMonthSelect` / `CalendarYearSelect` help (they are native-select style, so likely not used); the panel is ours, driven by `month` / `onMonthChange`.
- Tests first: `date-picker.panel.test.tsx` (both directions): heading name and `aria-expanded`; two presses to March 1987 from November 2026; every key above; Escape steps back and the popover stays open; disabled years and months read and not choosable; scroll position on open; axe clean.
