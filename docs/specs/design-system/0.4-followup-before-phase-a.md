# Spec 0.4 follow-up: what the design system owes before the first Phase A screen

Status: Approved (owner, 2026-11-23, with the sheet) · Date: 2026-11-23 · Risk tier: T1 (no patient data; synthetic names only) · Owner: product owner
Follows [spec 0.4](0.4-design-system-foundations.md) (phase closed, #119); slices continue the 0.4 table: 0.4-12 and 0.4-13 (two slices, owner 2026-11-23). Decisions: OD-61, OD-62, OD-64, OD-66; new OD-67 (autocomplete), OD-68 (month and year view), OD-69 (typed date), OD-70 (time chooser). Sheet: "Shifa Components (proposals)", page "Before Phase A · 0.4-12 and 0.4-13" (four boards), approved 2026-11-23.

## Goal
Close the gaps a Phase A list, form or booking screen would hit on day one: the narrow-width faults found in the phase walkthrough, a Date Picker that reaches a birth date decades back in two presses and takes a typed date segment by segment, a Time Input whose clock button opens a slot chooser, and the backlog items the owner placed before Phase A (Arabic search folding and announcements, staff-side autocomplete, the UI SDK dev-build history, icon contrast pairs, small records, the Solar licence in Flutter, hand-typed names, an LTR story with Arabic copy).

## User stories
- Receptionist → types «14031987» into the birth date → sees 14/03/1987 and «السبت 14 آذار 1987 · 39 سنة».
- Receptionist → opens the calendar, presses the heading, picks 1987 and آذار → is on March 1987 in two presses.
- Receptionist → presses the clock button of an appointment time → picks «10:30» from the clinic's 15-minute slots in one press.
- Receptionist → searches «احمد» → finds «أحمد» and a name stored with harakat; a screen reader hears the result count in Arabic.

## Acceptance criteria
Slice 0.4-12 (narrow width and small items):
1. DataTable: a header wider than its column is cut with an ellipsis; when (and only when) it is cut, hover after 500 ms and focus at once show the full text in the Tooltip; the full text stays the column's accessible name; sorting is unchanged (OD-66).
2. Pagination switches to the compact form («الصفحة 3 من 13», two arrows, the summary on a second line) by a container query when its container is under 430 px; every button stays inside the region at 320 px and at 200 % zoom (OD-66).
3. Toolbar: a button label stays on one line, at most 150 px, with an ellipsis; the icon never shrinks; the full label is the accessible name and shows in the Tooltip (OD-66).
4. Pagination's rows-per-page Select is at least 88 px wide (76 px compact) from a new size token, showing «100» and the chevron (OD-66).
5. Combobox (`matchesWordStart`) and Select typeahead fold Arabic as OD-40 does (NFC, harakat and tatweel removed, alef forms to ا, word-final ة → ه and ى → ي, Arabic-Indic digits to Western): «احمد» finds «أحمد», «فاطمه» finds «فاطمة». One shared folding function, also used by the command palette.
6. Combobox announces its result count in Arabic in RTL («3 نتائج», «لا نتائج») and in English in LTR.
7. Staff-side autocomplete (OD-67): `Input`, `Textarea` and `FormField` default to `autocomplete="off"`; a field opts in with an explicit token (`name`, `tel`, `bday`…), used only on the patient's own booking forms; recorded in the design doc and `/ui-component`; `password-input.test.tsx` asserts `current-password`.
8. UI SDK dev history (P-N2): `design-system-bundle.test.ts` fails if the bundle contains `ui-sdk.devtools` or `__UI_SDK__`; the design doc says a staging build never runs with `NODE_ENV=development`.
9. Icon contrast (C-5): `color.icon.*` has pairs on `brand.primary-soft`, `brand.accent-soft` and `selection.subtle`, and a schema rule fails when a text surface has no icon pair.
10. Records (C-6, C-7): `icons/sources.ts` names the generated file; `dependency-licences.json` lists `@iconify-json/solar` under CC BY 4.0.
11. Flutter (P-N7): `registerShifaLicences` (renamed from `registerShifaFontLicences`) also registers Solar's CC BY 4.0 licence; a widget test reads it from `LicenseRegistry`.
12. Names (P-N1): every person's name in web stories, tests and previews and in `shifa_ui` goldens and tests comes from the generator (`test/fixtures.ts`; a Dart fixture for Flutter); a repo scan test refuses the four hand-typed names listed in the backlog.
13. Pagination's `Zoom200` story in LTR·English shows English copy.

Slice 0.4-13 (date and time choosers):
14. Date Picker heading is a button («تشرين الثاني 2026» + caret, `aria-expanded`); it opens, inside the same popover, one panel (OD-68 B): a year list at the logical start (listbox, scrolled to the chosen year or the current one) beside a 3 × 4 grid of Levantine month names (OD-61). Choosing a month shows its days. Up/Down move a year, Page Up/Down ten years, Home/End the first and last; typed digits jump to a year; in the month grid arrows follow the reading direction; Enter chooses; Escape goes back one step, never closing the popover. Years and months outside `min`/`max` are disabled and still read.
15. Date Picker typed entry is three segments day / month / year (OD-69 A), left to right in both directions, each a named spinbutton («اليوم»، «الشهر»، «السنة») with placeholders «يوم», «شهر», «سنة»: a digit that cannot start a two-digit value moves on («4» → 04); `/`, `-`, `.` or a space move to the next segment; Backspace in an empty segment goes back; Up/Down step; Arabic-Indic digits are made Western; pasting `14/03/1987`, `14-3-1987`, `14.3.1987` or `1987-03-14` fills all three; the value is ISO.
16. Errors in Arabic (and English in LTR), shown on blur or when a complete date is impossible: «شباط 1987 فيه 28 يوماً», «أكمل التاريخ: السنة ناقصة», «اختر تاريخاً من 23/11/2026 فصاعداً», «اختر تاريخاً حتى …» (a `maxMessage` override gives «تاريخ الميلاد لا يكون بعد اليوم»), «اكتب السنة بأربعة أرقام: 1987» (two-digit years are never guessed). A complete date shows its weekday and Levantine month under the field.
17. Segments and calendar stay in sync: a typed date opens the calendar on its month; a chosen day fills the segments.
18. UI SDK `DateInput` passes an RTL gate (segment order, arrows, paste, Arabic-Indic digits) before it is used; if it fails, the segments are built on Time Input's parts, and the gate result is recorded in the slice file.
19. Time Input's clock icon is a button («اختيار الوقت»; Enter, Space or Alt+Down from a segment) opening a slot grid (OD-70 B): times at `step` minutes (default 15) between `min` and `max`, four columns, grouped (ليلاً، صباحاً، بعد الظهر، مساءً; only the groups that have slots), each time left to right, «ص/م» on the 12-hour clock; one press chooses, fills the segments and closes; focus opens on the chosen time or the next one after now; arrows follow the reading direction; Escape closes and returns focus to the button. Typing stays the main way.

Both slices: stories in RTL and LTR, light and dark, both densities, with axe and screenshot baselines; the design system synced after merge.

## Edge cases
- Header cut only at some widths: the tooltip follows the measured overflow, re-checked on resize.
- Pagination inside a container with no size: the compact form is the fallback.
- Date Picker with only `min` or only `max`: the year list runs 120 years back or 10 years ahead of today from the given end; with neither, 1900 to today + 10.
- 29/02 on a non-leap year is the impossible-date error; 31 in a 30-day month too.
- Time grid with no `min`/`max` shows 96 slots (00:00–23:45 at 15) in four groups; `step` that does not divide 60 is refused in development.
- Offline: nothing here calls the network.

## Permissions, money, abuse, privacy
No roles, money or server calls. Privacy: criterion 7 stops shared reception browsers from storing patient names and phones; criterion 8 keeps typed values out of a page-global history. No real names anywhere (criterion 12).
