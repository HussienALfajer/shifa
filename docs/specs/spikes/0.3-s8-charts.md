# Spec 0.3-S8: Charts — odontogram, perio entry and skeleton chart prototypes tested with doctors (spike)

Status: Approved (owner, 2026-11-17) · Date: 2026-11-17 · Risk tier: T1 (clinical chart rules: tooth numbering, surfaces, laterality; throwaway code, no backend, no patient data) · Owner: product owner
Requirements: DEN-01 (permanent only), DEN-02, DEN-03, DEN-04, DEN-05, DEN-06, DEN-07, DEN-08 (subset), DEN-09, DEN-11 (undo only), DEN-15, DEN-18 (PD, GM, CAL, BOP), DEN-19 (auto-advance, keypad, thresholds), ORT-01, ORT-02, ORT-03, ORT-05 · Decisions: [ADR-0011](../../adr/0011-design-system-base-ui.md) (unchanged), ADR-0023, ADR-0026, OD-17, OD-19 (stays open), OD-20 (stays open), OD-47; result in the dental and orthopedics domain docs

## Goal
The three clinical charts are the product's differentiator and its largest usability risk: new users completed only 43% of charting tasks correctly in legacy dental products [RN-12 §Q1], and laterality is the most common orthopedic listing error [RN-13 §11]. The domain docs answer these with rules (tooth first, two palettes, missing vs extracted, explicit side chips) that no doctor has tried. This spike builds interactive prototypes of the odontogram, the six-site perio entry and the skeleton chart, and measures scripted tasks with local doctors before Phase B and C commit to the design. Owner decisions (2026-11-17, OD-47): Claude Design replaces Figma for this spike (design exploration as live artboards); the tested build is a static web bundle in the repository so that every doctor uses the same version, the build runs with the network off and it logs each task; 3 dentists and 2 orthopedic surgeons, in person, moderated by the owner; laptop and 10-inch tablet; pass mark 80% unassisted task success.

## User stories
- Owner (المالك) → reviews two design directions per chart in Claude Design and picks one → the tested build follows that direction.
- Dentist (طبيب الأسنان) → charts findings and planned treatment on a synthetic patient (مخطط الأسنان) and enters a perio exam (مخطط اللثة) → the owner sees where the design fails.
- Orthopedic surgeon (طبيب العظمية) → selects sites with a side (الجهة) and draws pain on the skeleton chart (مخطط الهيكل) → the owner sees whether side and level are ever wrong.
- Owner → reads the spike report → knows which chart rules are confirmed, which change, and what Phase B and C must build.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Design exploration:** one Claude Design project holds at least two directions per chart as live Arabic RTL artboards, at laptop (1366×768) and tablet (1280×800) sizes. The owner picks one direction per chart; the pick and a screenshot of each direction are recorded in the slice file. No Figma file is created.
2. **Tested build:** `spikes/s8-charts` is a static bundle with no backend that runs from the owner's laptop with the network off, on the laptop and on a 10-inch tablet over the local network. It makes no network request outside its own origin and stores nothing after the session is exported.
3. **Anatomical orientation:** in the RTL page the charts are not mirrored: the patient's right is on the viewer's left in the odontogram (18→11 then 21→28), the perio grid and the anterior skeleton view (reversed in the posterior view). FDI numbers and measurements use Western digits; the report records what doctors say about digits (input to OD-20).
4. **Odontogram:** permanent dentition in FDI; anatomical view with five-surface occlusal view; tooth first, then action; multi-select; two separate palettes, "existing" and "planned"; Missing and Extracted as distinct items with distinct symbols; surfaces {M, O, D, B, L} with I/F/P aliases by tooth position; fixed status colours; a bridge wizard (abutments, pontic); undo. Seed items: caries, missing, extracted, composite filling, crown, bridge, RCT.
5. **Perio entry:** six sites per tooth for 28 teeth; probing depth and gingival margin with CAL computed (CAL = PD + GM, recession positive); bleeding per site; one fixed auto-advance path; keyboard entry on the laptop and an on-screen keypad on the tablet; values of 10 or more; red highlight at 4 mm and above; missing teeth skipped.
6. **Skeleton chart:** anterior and posterior skeleton; zoom panels for hand, foot and spine (C1–S1); a selection is not complete without a side chip (right, left, bilateral, midline); pain marks with six symptom types by colour and pattern, point or radiating, intensity 0–10. Art is self-drawn or public domain, with the source recorded.
7. **Tablet:** on the tablet viewport every interactive target is at least 44×44 CSS px and every scripted task can be finished by touch alone.
8. **Scripted tasks:** the build presents each task in Arabic on a synthetic patient, one at a time, and scores the final chart state against the expected state. Dental: D1 mark 36 missing and 46 extracted; D2 chart an existing MOD composite on 26; D3 record caries on the distal of 21; D4 plan a three-unit bridge 45–47 with 46 as pontic; D5 enter a full perio exam (168 depths, plus margins and bleeding for one quadrant) from a printed sheet; D6 on the tablet, repeat D2 and one sextant of D5. Orthopedic: O1 select the left knee; O2 select the PIP joint of the right middle finger; O3 select L4–L5; O4 draw burning pain radiating down the back of the left leg at intensity 7 and numbness on the outer left foot; O5 on the tablet, repeat O1 and O4.
9. **Session log:** per attempt the build records task, device, duration, undo count, wrong-tooth or wrong-side selections, final result and a moderator mark "assisted"; the owner exports one JSON file per participant. A participant is a code (`D1`–`D3`, `O1`–`O2`) with specialty, experience band and current software; no name, clinic, phone, audio or video.
10. **Sessions:** the owner runs five in-person sessions (3 dentists, about 45 minutes; 2 orthopedic surgeons, about 25 minutes) from a moderator script (`docs/spikes/s8-moderator-script.md`): two minutes of free exploration, no demonstration, help only when the participant gives up (the attempt then counts as assisted). Each session ends with five rating questions and free comments.
11. **Pass mark:** an attempt succeeds when the final state is correct and unassisted; D5 succeeds with at most 3 wrong cells. Each chart (odontogram D1–D4 and D6; perio D5; skeleton O1–O5) passes at 80% or more successful attempts with no task failed by more than half of its participants. Durations are reported, with no threshold.
12. **Revision round:** a chart that misses the pass mark gets one design revision and a retest of its failed tasks with at least two participants (new ones where possible; repeats are named in the report). A second miss is reported as a failure and carried to the phase that builds the chart.
13. **Result:** `docs/spikes/s8-charts.md` answers the questions below with the numbers, the participants' comments and the build commit; the anonymised logs are committed next to it. `docs/domain/dental.md` and `docs/domain/orthopedics.md` get the confirmed and changed rules; the roadmap is updated. The report says whether Claude Design should also replace Figma for the design system; that change would need a new ADR superseding the Figma line of ADR-0011 and closes OD-19.

## Questions the spike must answer
- Do the tooth-first rule, the two palettes and the Missing/Extracted split lift first-use success well above 43%?
- Is the three-unit bridge wizard understood without help?
- How long does a full six-site perio exam take by keyboard and by keypad, and is the auto-advance path the one dentists expect?
- Does any participant ever select the wrong side or level on the skeleton chart, and does the mandatory side chip prevent it?
- Are the charts usable by touch on a 10-inch tablet?
- Which Arabic terms for surfaces, statuses, symptom types and sides do doctors actually use?

## Edge cases
- Multi-select across the midline; action chosen with no tooth selected; a finding placed on a missing tooth (refused); a bridge with a missing abutment; anterior teeth (I/F/P aliases).
- Perio: correcting a value after auto-advance; a two-digit depth; a missing tooth in the path; negative margin.
- Skeleton: a midline site (spine) offers no right or left; changing the side after selection; a radiating mark that crosses regions; switching anterior and posterior views with marks present.
- A participant stops a session early: finished attempts count, the rest are recorded as not attempted.
- Offline: the build is offline by design. Permissions per role: not modelled.

## Money, abuse and privacy
- No real patient data (ADR-0023): one synthetic patient; the perio sheet is generated. Doctors must not enter a real patient's findings; the moderator script says so.
- Doctors are real people: only the participant code and three attributes are recorded; consent to take part is verbal; nothing that identifies a doctor or clinic enters the repository (ADR-0026) or the Claude Design project.
- The Claude Design project stays private to the owner's account and holds designs only.
- Never logged: free text typed by a participant (the build has no free-text field). Money and abuse: none.
